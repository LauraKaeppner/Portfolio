Professor Papillon

Help Professor Papillon build a beautiful butterfly sanctuary! Plant flowers, attract butterflies, and keep the balance of your ecosystem. But be careful, every decision affects your butterfly population. Will you create butterfly paradise or send the garden into chaos? The choice is yours!

This game was made for the Cozy Spring Game Jam 2025!
